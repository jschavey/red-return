FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will I be the first human on Mars?

In a sense.

What happens to the rest of me?

A lovely forest in Oregon. Shipping all of you to Mars would cost more than most of our clients' yachts, and they asked us not to bring that up.

Isn't sending Earth microbes to Mars against international treaty?

We prefer to think of the Outer Space Treaty as a living document. Like you, briefly.

What if the launch fails?

You will be complimentarily upgraded to Atmospheric Reentry Cremation. Many customers consider this the premium experience.

Will I actually help terraform Mars?

Your money will. You will mostly become a very expensive layer of sterilized dust. Which is, honestly, a legacy.

Can my family visit?

Yes. Tickets go on sale soon. They have been going on sale soon since we launched.
